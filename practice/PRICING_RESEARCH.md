# Pricing and viability research (November 2026)

Desk research from public sources. Many price figures come from clinic websites and
third-party price guides, so treat them as approximate and check them before quoting
them to anyone. USD figures use ≈ R18 / $.

---

| Provider | What it is | Price | Notes |

| Virgin Active Point, Longevity Suite | In-gym longevity assessment | **R2,800** (members) / **R3,600** (non-members), opening rate May–June 2026; pathology billed separately | Mass-market anchor. One-off, no ongoing doctor relationship |
| Life Extension Institute, Mayo Clinic (Johannesburg hospital, not the US Mayo Clinic) | Executive medical evaluation | **R6,115** (Mar 2026–Feb 2027); up to R3,511 claimable from some medical aids | Established, partly claimable. One-off, in person |

| Cape Longevity Clinic | Longevity clinic | Platinum membership **R10,000 / month**; 180-min BAHI circuit R2,500 | Closest local membership benchmark. In person, includes treatments |
| Netcare Longevity Centre, Johannesburg | Programmes and retreats | From ~$4,000 (aggregator figure, unverified) | Hospital-group backed |

**What this shows:** local longevity offerings are almost all **in person and
treatment-led** (drips, hormones, devices). One-off assessments cost R3,000–6,000. A
membership near R10,000 a month includes physical treatments. Nobody local clearly offers
what you're planning: **remote, doctor-led, whole-lifestyle programming with a
personal dashboard and no product sales.**

**What this shows:**
- **Blood testing is becoming a commodity:** the testing-only apps sell it for a few
  hundred dollars a year. Don't sell bloodwork itself. Sell your interpretation of the
  results and the plan.
- **Virtual, doctor-led memberships** cluster at **$250–900 a month**. Your planned
  Essential and Performance tiers (≈ $250 and $525) are squarely in the international range.
- **Above ~$1,000 a month**, buyers expect in-person diagnostics, imaging or treatments,
  none of which you'll offer.

## 2. Is the niche viable?

### For

- **You need very few clients.** 30–50 members is a full practice for one doctor.
  - South Africa has about **41,100 dollar millionaires** (Henley Africa Wealth Report 2025),
    plus many more high earners: specialists, executives, founders.
  - You need roughly 0.1% of them, and you can also serve South Africans abroad.
- **There's a real gap locally.** Local clinics are in person and treatment-led. A remote,
  independent (no product sales), whole-lifestyle doctor service with a dashboard is
  different, and it suits people who travel and have no time for clinic visits.
- **Demand is growing.** Wearables are mainstream (Oura passed $500m revenue in 2024, and
  Garmin posted record revenue in 2025). People have the data and want someone to make
  sense of it. Market estimates disagree widely, but all show growth.
- **Your credibility is the product.** You're a doctor who trains seriously and already
  built your own dashboard. Coaches can't match the medical side, and clinics can't match
  the training side.
- **Costs are low:** no rooms, no staff, no data hosting. Most of the fee is margin.

### Against

- **The local pool is small and shrinking.** South Africa has lost over 2,400 millionaires
  in a decade. Plan for international clients early (subject to registration rules).
- **Clients may leave once they've learned the basics.** After 3–6 months, many feel they
  "get it". You'll need a reason to stay: quarterly retests, new training blocks, travel
  and race plans, and a cheaper maintenance tier.
- **Your time is the ceiling.** A solo doctor at 20 client-hours a week tops out around
  R3–4m a year. Growth beyond that needs group programmes, company contracts or hiring
  coaches.
- **Marketing is limited.** HPCSA rules ban testimonials and superlatives. Growth will come
  from referrals, your network (doctors, executives) and educational content, which is
  slower than typical wellness marketing.
- **"Information-based and fully remote" looks less premium** than a clinic with scans
  and drips. Price it below the treatment-led clinics, not above them.
- **No data between calls.** That's good for privacy, but you can't spot problems early,
  so the value has to come from the calls and the plan.

### Verdict

**Viable as a small, premium, doctor-led service**, provided that:
1. it's priced **below in-person longevity clinics** and **at or slightly above good online coaching**;
2. you build **retention**: retests, programme blocks, a maintenance tier;
3. you test it fast: **5–10 paying founding clients within 90 days** of launch. If you can't
   find them through your own network at founding prices, rethink the offer before
   spending on marketing.

It's unlikely to become a large business without company contracts or other coaches.
As a high-margin practice of 30–50 clients alongside or instead of clinical work, it's
realistic.

## 3. Recommended pricing

Compared with the first draft, prices are **lower at the top and at the entry point**,
because the service is remote, information-based and has no treatments. Local anchors are
R3–6k for an assessment and R10k a month for an in-person membership. International
virtual memberships run $250–900 a month.

| | First draft | **Recommended** | ≈ USD (international list) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline Assessment (90-min Zoom, bloods request, results call, 12-week protocol) | R14,500 | **R8,500**, credited in full if they join within 30 days | **$650** |
| Essential: monthly Zoom | R4,500 / mo | **R3,500 / mo** | **$250 / mo** |
| Performance: Zoom every 2 weeks, written programme | R9,500 / mo | **R7,500 / mo** | **$450 / mo** |
| Executive: weekly Zoom, same-day replies, capped at 10–15 | R22,000 / mo | **R15,000 / mo** | **$950 / mo** |
| **New:** Maintenance, a 45-min Zoom every quarter plus a protocol refresh, for people leaving Essential or higher | none | **R1,500 / mo** (or R4,500 / quarter) | **$100 / mo** |

**Why:**
- **Assessment:** at R8,500 it sits above Mayo SA's R6,115 evaluation, which is in person but
  one-off with no plan, and below the first draft's R14,500, which was the biggest barrier.
  Crediting it towards membership turns it into a down payment.
- **International clients** pay roughly US virtual-membership prices, which are higher than
  the rand list. Charge them in USD.
- **Maintenance tier:** keeps people who would otherwise cancel, and keeps them in line for
  retests.
- **Labs:** always paid by the client directly to the lab. No mark-up and no handling of
  results through the app.

**Time and rate check** (your hours per client per month, including prep and messages):

| Tier | Price | Hours / month | Effective rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maintenance | R1,500 | ~0.4 h | ~R3,750 / h |
| Essential | R3,500 | ~1.25 h | ~R2,800 / h |
| Performance | R7,500 | ~2.25 h | ~R3,300 / h |
| Executive | R15,000 | ~4.5 h | ~R3,300 / h |

That's comparable to or above a private GP's hourly rate, for work you control from home.

**Example at about 20 client-hours a week:** 20 Essential + 15 Performance + 6 Executive +
10 Maintenance comes to **R287,500 / month (≈ R3.45m / year)**, plus assessments
(e.g. 4 a month ≈ R34,000). Costs are small: Zoom, booking, records system, indemnity.

**Other levers**
- Annual prepay: 2 months free.
- Founding 10: 30% off for 12 months.
- Doctors' rate: 15% off for fellow medical professionals. They're a referral engine and
  will judge you on substance.
- Company packages: 5+ people at Performance level, billed annually to the company, at 15% off.
