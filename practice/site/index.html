Method

Measure. Design. Track. Adjust.

Your GP, trainer, dietitian and wearable app each see part of the picture. Here one doctor sees all of it and decides what matters most for you this month.

01

Measure

Blood tests at a lab near you, simple measurements at home and your wearable history give an objective baseline before anything changes.

02

Design

A written 12-week protocol built around your calendar, travel and goals: training, nutrition targets, sleep window, caffeine plan, supplements.

03

Track

A dashboard app on your own phone that reads your wearable. Your health data stays on your device. Logging caffeine, protein and supplements takes about ten seconds a day.

04

Adjust

Regular video reviews with your doctor and retesting at 12 weeks. The plan changes when your data or your life does.

Questions

Good to know

Anything else? Ask when you book. The first call is free and lasts 15 minutes.

Is everything online?

Yes. Every consultation is a video call, so there are no clinic visits. You do blood tests at any pathology lab near you, and simple measurements like blood pressure and weight at home.

Can I join from outside South Africa?

The full medical service is currently for people living in South Africa, including South Africans travelling for work. A coaching-only option for international clients is planned.

Do you prescribe medication?

No. This is an information and advice service. If you need a prescription or treatment, we refer you to your GP or a specialist and, with your consent, share the relevant findings with them.

Does this replace my GP?

No. It complements your GP. With your consent, we share relevant results with your GP, and anything outside our scope is referred on.

Do I need a wearable?

It helps, but you don't need one to start. The app reads from Apple Health or Health Connect, so it works with Garmin, Whoop, Oura, Apple Watch, Polar, Samsung and most smart scales. Without a wearable, the dashboard uses your daily check-ins and training log.

What if my watch brand changes or blocks data access?

We set up your dashboard with you on a video call and adjust it until it works with your devices. If Garmin or another provider changes or blocks access to your data, we switch your dashboard to another source, such as Apple Health, Health Connect, another device or manual entry. Some measures may change or pause while we do.

Will you put me on lots of supplements?

Usually only a few. We recommend supplements only where your bloodwork or good evidence supports them, and point you to third-party-tested brands. We never sell supplements and take no commission on them.

How much time does it take each week?

About ten seconds a day to log caffeine, protein and supplements, plus your review call. The training itself fits around your diary.

Who sees my data?

Your wearable data and daily logs stay in the app on your own phone; we don't run a server that holds them. Blood results are discussed with you on video calls and are never put in the app. For company packages, your employer never sees your medical information.
